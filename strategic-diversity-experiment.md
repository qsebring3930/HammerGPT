# Stage 1/2 diversity experiment

`StrategicPlanner.propose(starting_condition, seed)` samples composable, coordinate-free gameplay rules through `semantic_organizations.py`, then compiles purposeful route intentions. It does not copy proposal 001 or use a trained neural checkpoint. Starting conditions constrain some strategic decisions; a local seeded RNG chooses remaining compatible decisions. There is no coordinate sampler, spatial embedder, floor generator or preference ranker in this experiment.

`benchmark_strategic_diversity.py` creates sixteen reproducible proposals: twelve independently constrained starting conditions and four seeds using the same unconstrained brief. No failed proposal is repaired or rerolled. All are run through the same semantic validator, with additional arrival witnesses for contact and early territory ownership. The rejection controls include unsupported Mid, redundant alternatives and purpose-free rejoining branches.

The comparison in `strategic_similarity.py` extracts nine families from the actual strategic representation: deployment, convergence, first contacts, site approaches, rotation, control function, flanks, recovery, and path equivalence. It excludes seed, title, raw IDs, rule labels and A/B identity. Equivalent duplicate intentions are collapsed before features are collected. Shared preparation and changed destinations are tactical relationships, not graph-complexity rewards. The equal-weight Jaccard similarity and 0.80 complete-link grouping threshold are exploratory assumptions, not human-calibrated strategic identity judgments.

Final batch: ten of sixteen proposals pass; all ten have different nine-family signatures under this comparison. Six are retained as failures, including missing two-site Mid consequences, purpose-free split deployment and reconnecting preparation/rotation alternatives. Eight of twelve constrained proposals and two of four unconstrained proposals pass. The accepted set has only two flank signatures and three recovery signatures, with shared site-interface scaffolding. These are specific Stage 1 limitations, even though the majority does not collapse under the exploratory similarity measure. The original five rejected radars still fail the semantic benchmark.

Run `.tools/training/Scripts/python.exe benchmark_strategic_diversity.py`. Results and side-by-side explanations are in `output/strategic-diversity-001/review.md`, with each complete blueprint, validation report and extracted feature set in its Pxx directory. The JSON pairwise report contains all comparisons, not only favorable examples.

Passing means internally supported *design contracts*. Timings, information, entry angles, exposure and ownership are intentions, not measured gameplay. Phase-specific movement availability is still an abstraction; teams and phases cannot act as physical barriers once embedding exists. The set tests rule-grammar expressiveness under deliberately different constraints, not a learned model's novelty rate. Four unconstrained seeds alone are insufficient for a distributional diversity claim.

All sixteen proposed plans remain coordinate-free. No proposal is embedded. Floor generation remains paused regardless of benchmark outcome. Existing radars and their geometry are untouched.
